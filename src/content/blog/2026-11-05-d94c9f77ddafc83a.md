You have no way to tell which model actually served your request. Anthropic's FlashProve attacks that with a zkSNARK proof that committed weights produced exactly those output tokens: a 400B-parameter MoE on a single H100, ~110 tokens/s proving, ~19MB per proof, verified on a CPU in about 17 seconds. The catch: roughly 72x the GPU time of plain vLLM, greedy decoding only, 1,024-token context. Code is out under Apache 2.0.
