Want to practise GPU scheduling but can't get a GPU? This one builds the whole multi-tenant setup on a single Linux box with no card in it. Kamaji gives each tenant its own control plane, HAMi's mock device plugin fakes an A100 80GB and two L4s, and the quota logic behaves: three pods at 20,000MB and 30% compute share one card, a fourth stays Pending. Enforcement isn't real, but the scheduling decisions are.
