A trillion parameters, 49B active, trained on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs in European datacenters — and the numbers that stand out aren't the coding ones. Mistral Large 4 solves 93% of Cybench challenges and hits 82% on vulnerability reproduction and patching, which they call the highest of any model. Weights drop at the end of the month, so "attacker with a capable security model" stops being a thought experiment.
