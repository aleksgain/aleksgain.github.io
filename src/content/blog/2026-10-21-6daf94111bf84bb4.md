Cloud account takeover used to end in crypto mining. Now it ends in your Gemini bill. Wiz traced intrusions that started with Google Search ads for “gcp” pointing at a fake login page, then went straight for billing: delete the iam.allowedPolicyMemberDomains org policy, add Gmail accounts as billing admins, link attacker-owned projects to your billing account, demote the real admins to viewers. Charges show up within 24-48 hours.
