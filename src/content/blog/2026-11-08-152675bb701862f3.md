Six model configurations sat within 1.5 points of each other around 74% on DeepSWE. Reword the task instructions a little and that cluster fans out across 23 points. MaxxBench changed 89 DeepSWE tasks — reference patches grew by a median of 17 lines — and the spread across 15 configurations went from 20.9 to 48.3 points. Gemini 3.8 Flash fell from 73.8% to 53.4%. Worth remembering next time a leaderboard looks tight.
