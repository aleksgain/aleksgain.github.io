Client analytics tell you what a member tapped. Backend traces tell you what the services did. LinkedIn joined the two on OpenTelemetry: a traceId per request plus a "pageForestId" linking everything from one page context, head sampling coordinated so client and server make the same keep-or-drop call, then Kafka into ClickHouse. Their estimate that up to 90% of backend tracing volume is really debug-level data is the argument for owning it.
