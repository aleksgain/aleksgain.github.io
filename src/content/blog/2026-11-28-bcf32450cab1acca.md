Residual connections weight every earlier layer equally, which is an odd default once you say it out loud. Google's DeepCrossAttention swaps that fixed add for learnable depth-wise cross-attention over prior layers, and the numbers hold up: baseline transformer quality in up to 5x fewer training steps on C4 and LM1B, and 30 layers instead of 42 to get perplexity under 14 on LM1B. Cost: under 0.2% extra parameters, though memory bandwidth goes up.
