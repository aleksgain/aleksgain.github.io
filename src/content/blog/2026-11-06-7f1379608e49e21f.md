Microsoft is designing its Arm chips on its Arm chips, and actually published the numbers: 1.30x to 4.15x the per-logical-core throughput of Cobalt 100 across five EDA tools, with Cadence Xcelium at 4.15x and Synopsys PrimeTime at 2.03x. Cobalt 200 is 128 Neoverse V3 cores, no hyperthreading, 3MB of L2 each. The quietly impressive part: the tools ran unmodified, because they already ran on Cobalt 100.
