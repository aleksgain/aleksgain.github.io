A genuinely new browser engine that you can download instead of compile. Ladybird hit alpha for macOS and Linux — written from scratch, not a fork of Chromium, Gecko or WebKit, and funded entirely by donations through a 501(c)(3) that aims to hold 18 months of runway. It speaks WebDriver and the Firefox DevTools protocol, so testing against it is realistic. The team is blunt: sites will break, it will crash, no security promises yet 😄
