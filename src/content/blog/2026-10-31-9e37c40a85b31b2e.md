The number that stuck with me isn't from SRE at all. A Lancet study found endoscopists' unassisted polyp detection fell from 28% to 22% after a stretch of AI-assisted work. Bainbridge made this argument in 1983: automate the routine and you leave humans only the rare, high-stakes failures they have had no practice for. The suggested fix is deliberately inefficient — hand some incidents to a human even when the AI could close them.
