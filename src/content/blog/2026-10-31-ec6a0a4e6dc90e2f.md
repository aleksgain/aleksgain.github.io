At least nine AI developer platforms disclosed incidents in October, from Fireworks AI on the 6th to Northflank on the 30th. Wiz then followed the stolen model keys: first unauthorized call within 30 minutes of the credential access, then one case burning roughly 1.7 million Bedrock calls in 13 hours. Two cases together ran up over $100k at list price. If your platform stores a customer's model keys, you are storing their credit card 😅
