PlanetScale, the team behind Vitess, has introduced Neki, a sharded Postgres service that runs unmodified Postgres on every shard. You keep extensions and full SQL, and routers spread queries across shard groups by shard key. It's in platform preview and not recommended for production yet. Worth watching if you're close to the limits of a single Postgres primary.
