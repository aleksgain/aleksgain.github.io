The honest incident write-ups are the useful ones. Alpine patched GitLab for CVE-2026-97229 (CVSS 10.0) and was still vulnerable twice over: first installing 19.3.4 instead of 19.3.5, then finding 19.3.6 didn't help because the real fix was a Ruby patch GitLab ships only in its own packages. An attacker crashed workers 8 times and pulled the image to tune the exploit. No tampering found, but logs were missing — so they rebuilt from scratch.
