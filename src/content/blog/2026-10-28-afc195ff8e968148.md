"One CPU" is a lie your scheduler tells you: across Uber's ~5M cores, single-threaded performance varies almost 2x between oldest and newest servers. So Odin allocates Normalized Compute Units: each server type carries a performance factor (old baseline 100, newest ~190), and a workload gets fewer fast cores or more slow ones. Across 1.4M autoscaled cores, Kafka's utilization spread fell from ~8 points to ~3 and total allocation dropped 8%.
