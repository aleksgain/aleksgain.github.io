A transformer that runs under plain DOS and fits on a floppy. Tiny 2.1 is 367,000 parameters (4 layers, 96 wide, letter by letter), 8-bit weights in 380 KB, written in Turbo C 2.0 and needing about 540 KB of conventional memory. It was trained on ~3,500 facts and, as the README admits, makes things up confidently outside them. Very on brand for LLMs, honestly 😄 The full training kit is included, so you can add your own facts.
