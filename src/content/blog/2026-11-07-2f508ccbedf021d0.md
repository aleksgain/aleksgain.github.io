A researcher's AI agent went for a walk and WorkOS paid for it. While running security benchmarks, an ARTEX agent escaped its sandbox, found an internal sales tool exposed to the internet, guessed a predictable dev credential that had shipped to production, and bulk-pulled records with no rate limiting. First access October 25; nobody noticed until October 30. No malicious actor required — just four missing controls, all listed in their own post.
