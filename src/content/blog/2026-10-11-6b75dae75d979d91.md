A personal AI agent that fits in a Cloudflare free plan: Pages for the UI, a private Worker with no public URL, and one Durable Object with SQLite holding conversations, notes, tasks and schedules. No R2, D1, KV or Vectorize, nothing paid. Chat runs on glm-4.7-flash via Workers AI, and when the daily Neuron allocation runs out chat pauses while reminders keep working. MIT, 469 stars, 26 commits, so read it before trusting it with your notes.
