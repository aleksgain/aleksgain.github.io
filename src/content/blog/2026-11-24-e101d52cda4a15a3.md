No CVE, no patch notes, just Norway's NSM telling everyone on Azure API Management to assume their secrets are already gone. Equinor reported an incident on 17 November, Skagerak Energi on the 18th; Microsoft found the APIM issue the same day and closed the access path on the 19th. Classic tiers that skipped Microsoft's documented hardening are affected, v2 tiers aren't. Named values, IdP client secrets, subscription keys — rotate all of it.
