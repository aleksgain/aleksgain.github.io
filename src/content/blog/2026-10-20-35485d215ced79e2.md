Seven preview rounds — the longest cycle of any Project Loom feature — and StructuredTaskScope is finally final in JDK 28. The good news for anyone who already took the plunge: no API changes since JDK 27's seventh preview, so migration is dropping --enable-preview and recompiling. GA is March 2027. Plenty of time to stop hand-rolling ExecutorService shutdown logic and let a failed subtask cancel its siblings for you.
