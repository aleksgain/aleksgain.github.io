Go audit the short links in your old posts. Someone has been re-registering expired bit.ly back-halves, so a 2011 Burger King tweet now resolves to a Wikipedia article on Nazi concentration camps. No account was hacked — the link changed owner. Of 97 Bitly links checked in old Trump tweets, only 40 were still the originals. Bitly's own help pages say back-halves "cannot be re-used", which is now demonstrably untrue.
