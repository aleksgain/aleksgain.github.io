A guardrail only covers the path you thought of. OpenAI blocked cross-model replay of encrypted reasoning in August; Xint simply asked the same model to compact its own reasoning and copy it out verbatim, with tabs instead of spaces to slip past the policy filter. Fixed server-side on October 23, $6,000 bounty, only the researchers' own puzzles involved. The worry: any step that decrypts reasoning and then follows user instructions can leak it.
