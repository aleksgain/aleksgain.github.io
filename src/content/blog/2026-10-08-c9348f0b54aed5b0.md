OpenSSH 10.6 breaks two things on purpose, and both are worth knowing before the upgrade lands in your base images. The LZ77 compression coder is disabled because a chosen-plaintext attack can recover secrets across channels through the shared dictionary, and ssh now refuses command-line usernames containing $ or \ to stop shell injection via ProxyCommand. Also in this one: a hybrid post-quantum signature type, and KDF rounds up from 24 to 32.
