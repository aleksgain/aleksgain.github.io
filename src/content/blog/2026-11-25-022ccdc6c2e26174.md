The "VMs are legacy" story keeps not happening. New CNCF and Linux Foundation research (492 practitioners, September) puts an average 53% of production applications on VMs — 64% at organisations over 10,000 people. More interesting: of the teams growing their VM footprint, 34% name AI and agentic workloads as a reason. When an agent is executing generated code, a container boundary starts to feel thin. MicroVM sandboxing: 20% using, 30% planning.
