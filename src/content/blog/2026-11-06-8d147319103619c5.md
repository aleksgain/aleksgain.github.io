Two reasonable decisions from 2012 just collided. musl's crypt() returns "*" when it fails, and it fails on passwords over 256 bytes. Alpine stores "*" as the password field for passwordless accounts. So sshd compares "*" to "*", they match, and a 257-byte password logs you into a key-only root. Fix is OpenSSH 10.6p2, or PasswordAuthentication no right now 😅
