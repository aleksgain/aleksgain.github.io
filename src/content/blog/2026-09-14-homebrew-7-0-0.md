Homebrew 7.0.0 is less a feature drop and more a "who do we still have your back on" release. `brew vulns` now checks installed packages against an advisory database, and Linux sandboxing swaps Bubblewrap for Landlock with zero extra deps. The one to actually plan around: Intel Macs drop to Tier 3 (no new prebuilt bottles, support ends September 2027), and macOS 10.15 and earlier are dropped outright. Check this before your next CI image rebuild.
