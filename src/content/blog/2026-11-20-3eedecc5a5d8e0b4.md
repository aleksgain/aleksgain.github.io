If you run PHP, this one isn't optional: 17 CVEs, seven of them critical at CVSS 9.2, each a path to unauthenticated RCE. The headliner, phpAfterShell (CVE-2026-126714), abuses non-atomic session file writes — and file-based sessions are the default. Wiz hasn't seen exploitation yet and most of the criticals need non-default config, but PHP still runs ~70% of sites with a known server-side language. Patched: 8.5.13 / 8.4.28 / 8.3.37 / 8.2.36.
