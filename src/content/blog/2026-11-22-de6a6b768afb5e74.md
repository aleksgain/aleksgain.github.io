17 million lines of Lean. The Lean and Mathlib communities say they've verified Levent Alpöge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis: the theorem matches Mathlib's existing RiemannHypothesis statement, dozens of people compiled it clean, and an inert export was re-checked by Lean's kernel plus third-party checkers that have proofs they cannot accept a false proof. Nobody has explained the new maths yet. The toolchain got there first.
