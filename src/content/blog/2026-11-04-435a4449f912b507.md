Worth reading the methods section before reposting the chart. The claim doing the rounds is that GPT-6 Astra improved Qwen3.5-0.8B "just by looking at raw weights": MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1% while editing about 0.016% of them. Matthew Honnibal points out the headline run used Python and up to 50 batches of practice problems, and the raw-weights-only runs were on 17K–172K-parameter nets. Real ML engineering, just not what people think.
