Same model, 65.5% to 74.2% on DeepSWE across eight harnesses — and the minimal ones won. Manju Rajashekhar's QCon SF keynote makes the case that the harness, not the model, is the operating system: context is virtual memory, tools are system calls, permissions decide what the agent is allowed to want. The line that stuck with me: approval prompts don't save you, because "a tired gatekeeper is a rubber stamp."
