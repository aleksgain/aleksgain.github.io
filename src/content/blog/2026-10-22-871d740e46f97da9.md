litellm 1.107.0 shipped a credential stealer, and the delivery is the nasty part: a cook.pth file means it runs in every Python process on the host, whether or not anything imports litellm. It went after SSH keys, AWS/Azure/gcloud creds, Vault and Terraform secrets, and tokens for Claude Code, Cursor and Copilot. PyPI pulled the release in about two hours; the Docker tags, latest included, stayed bad for another hour.
