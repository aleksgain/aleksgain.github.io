Your most trusted browser extension is still attack surface. A page could leak uBlock Origin's moz-extension UUID, load the element picker in an invisible iframe, then postMessage its way to injecting a *trusted* filter — i.e. arbitrary JS on any site. Root cause is the boring one: the picker accepted messages from any page. Fixed with a secret token, tracked as GHSA-cmqw-9r2w-6wv2. Found with an LLM, verified by a human.
