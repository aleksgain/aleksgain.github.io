Same query, same laptop, 18.8s down to 7.7s, and the SQL didn't change. DuckDB 2.0's alpha splits downloading from decoding: a dedicated pool of threads keeps dozens of row groups in flight while the workers only decode, instead of 18 workers each taking turns waiting on S3. The test counts 228 million rows out of a 2.2 GB Parquet file over a home connection. Overlapping I/O with compute is old advice, and it still pays like this.
