The CSI driver is the layer nobody puts in the threat model, and it quietly holds credentials for every array behind it. Two max-severity flaws in Dell Container Storage Modules hand over storage backend admin credentials for all registered arrays (CVE-2026-63688) and full control of the authorization service (CVE-2026-63692). Four more criticals get root on cluster nodes and bypass Kubernetes controls to read secrets. Fix is CSM 1.18.0.
