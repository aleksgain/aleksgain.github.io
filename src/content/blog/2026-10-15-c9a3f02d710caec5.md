If your firewall's management interface is reachable from anywhere you don't control, today is the day to fix that. Palo Alto confirmed "very limited exploitation" of CVE-2026-0322 (CVSS 8.6), an authenticated RCE that runs code as root on PA-Series, VM-Series and Panorama. It's already in CISA's KEV with an October 17 federal deadline, and there is no workaround - patch, or lock the interface down to trusted IPs.
