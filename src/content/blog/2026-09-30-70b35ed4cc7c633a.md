If you've built on the official MCP Python SDK, check your version. It didn't validate the authorization server's identity, so a malicious MCP server could talk a client into handing over its OAuth client credentials, then mint tokens with whatever scopes your app was granted. Fixed in 1.30.0 and 2.2.0 (affected: 1.9.1-1.29.1 and 2.0.0-2.1.1). Upgrading alone won't save you - that client secret is long-lived, so rotate it too.
