Mermaid.js is 1.6 MB gzipped and 23 direct dependencies to draw a flowchart. Pele does the same diagram types (Mermaid 12.1.0 syntax) in 230 kB with zero deps, and renders in 0.05-0.3 ms instead of 6-82 ms. The bit I like: theming is CSS variables, so colours change without re-rendering. MIT, originally written for Obsidian. Worth a look if diagrams are bloating your docs bundle.
