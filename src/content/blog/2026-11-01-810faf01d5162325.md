Jepsen reports rarely end with "migrate to other databases as soon as possible". ParticleDB's does. 96 issues, and all but three showed up on healthy single nodes — no partitions needed. My favourite: `WHERE 4 = 5` matched the row with primary key 5. Aborted transactions could commit, and the newest release candidate still loses committed writes in its default mode. Orkes paid for this analysis, which is its own kind of brave.
