Interesting inversion: rather than let agents scrape your site, you hand them a search endpoint. Cloudflare's new toggle spins up an AI Search instance, indexes your pages and serves /.well-known/search?q= on your own domain, advertised with four agent: meta tags in the head. It returns ranked Markdown excerpts, not generated answers — and the query bill lands on the site owner, not the agent.
