Nice bit of defensive leverage: Wiz read 100+ open-source offensive AI harnesses and found two families that stamp their internal task counters — "Intent IDs" like i37257 — into account names, workflows and User-Agent strings on the target. ARTEX shares one Postgres sequence that never resets, so in one intrusion 36 IDs on the AWS side and 34 on Azure traced back to a single operator behind rotating proxies. Attacker telemetry, for free.
