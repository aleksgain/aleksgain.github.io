Turns out the answer to "how do we let agents call internal services" is the thing we've already built three times: a gateway. Uber's MCP Gateway fronts 800+ MCP servers and 5,000 tools, generating MCP schemas straight out of Protobuf and Thrift definitions — and newly discovered tools stay disabled until the owning team reviews and enables them. That default-off bit is the actual lesson here 😄
