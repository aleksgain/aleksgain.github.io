The interesting part of how Anthropic reviews Claude Code's own PRs isn't that an AI reviews them — it's that they cap the loop. After three rounds it asks the author to reconsider the implementation. PRs passing after a single review-and-fix round went from 65% to 78%, those needing five or more rounds fell from 8.9% to 1.5%, and reverts dropped over 40%. Risk tiers set how many humans sign off; CI workflow files always need one.
