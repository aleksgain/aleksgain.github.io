AWS finally moved the Lambda ceiling: 15 minutes to 30 for on-demand Event Functions, every commercial region, standard pricing, no opt-in. That cap had stood since 2018. Before you crank it up, two things bite — an SQS trigger wants a visibility timeout of at least 6x the function timeout (3 hours at 30 minutes), and a NAT gateway still drops idle connections at 350 seconds. A 30-minute run also bills for all 30 at your configured memory.
