"Assume all environment variables have been decrypted. Disable/rotate all keys." That was Northflank's advice to its customers on 30 October, after a Wiz alert landed at 11:43 UTC and network policy patches went out at 12:50 and 13:05. Multiple customers have since reported unauthorized use of their AI API keys. Still unsaid: how the attackers got in, or how many customers were hit. Audit logs are now on for everyone, free tier included.
