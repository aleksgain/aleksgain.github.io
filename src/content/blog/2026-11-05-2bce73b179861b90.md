Patch-today material if you run Debian or Ubuntu: CVE-2026-109518 lets anyone who can tamper with your HTTP package traffic swap a verified .deb for their own, which installs as root. The attacker forces redirects and transient server errors until APT retries a download, then overrides hardlinks with their payload. Retries have been on by default since APT 2.3.2, so "I never configured that" is no defence. Fixes span 1.2.36 through 3.2.1.
