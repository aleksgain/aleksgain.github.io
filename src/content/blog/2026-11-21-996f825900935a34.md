Two of five Argo CD Applications ran new code against the old schema for 36 to 44 minutes, and both reported Synced and Healthy with "successfully synced (all tasks run)". The cause is self-heal: after a deny sync window closes, a level-1 refresh compares the recorded revision against itself, sees no change, and fires a partial sync that skips your migration hook. Reproduced in 5 of 6 lab runs. If your migrations are sync hooks, read this one 🫠
