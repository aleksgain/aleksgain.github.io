The pitch isn't “Go was the wrong call”, it's Wasm - compile the Go compiler to Wasm and you ship a garbage collector with it. tsrs is a Rust port of TypeScript from Cloudflare, VoidZero and Oxc, built with the TypeScript team rather than against it: up to 8x faster than TS 7.0 on about 70% less memory, and some of its optimizations landed upstream in the 7.1 beta. Experimental, npx tsrs, and they're firm that nobody is forking TypeScript.
