Rebuilding a 20-million-record DNS zone because one record changed works fine right up until it doesn't. Cloudflare's Zone Builder now plans the smallest build it can prove safe: 93% of builds skip the full rebuild, average working memory dropped from 49 GiB to 7 GiB, and the worst p99 delay spike went from 14.6 minutes to 1.21. A good reminder that "do less work" still beats "buy more RAM".
