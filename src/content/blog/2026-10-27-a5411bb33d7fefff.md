Two CVSS 9.9 sandbox escapes in Wasmtime, and the ARM64 one has been sitting there since ARM64 support landed in 2020. A module with a br_table of more than ~256,000 targets pushes the default-case branch past ARM64's ~1 MiB reach, and the engine "can be forced to jump to arbitrary code." The annoying part: as of Oct 26 GitHub's advisory DB had reviewed only the other CVE, so Dependabot stays quiet. Rebuild on 36.0.19, 48.0.7, 49.0.4 or 50.0.0.
