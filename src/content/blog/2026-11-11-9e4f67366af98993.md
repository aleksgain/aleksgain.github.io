Your stale TODO comments are now a performance problem. Google traced hundreds of millions of agent steps: 13% of sessions put an inline TODO/FIXME in front of an agent, and in 6% of those it went off and worked that bug unprompted — 41% of the time on a ticket already closed. Those detours cost 70% more steps, 77% more tokens, 105% more wait time. Their answer: a cleanup agent, 95%+ of its PRs accepted.
