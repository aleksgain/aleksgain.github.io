If your clusters still run kube-proxy in ipvs mode, there's now a clock on it: deprecated in Kubernetes 1.35, removal planned for 1.40. nftables has been GA since 1.33, is feature-complete and performs about as well, so for most people this is flipping mode: ipvs to mode: nftables in the ConfigMap (kernel 5.13+). Extra nudge: Red Hat is dropping the iptables API from RHEL, and ipvs leans on iptables anyway.
