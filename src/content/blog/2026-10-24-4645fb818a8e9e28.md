Everyone patched GitLab. Almost nobody patched the actual bug. Those four GitLab RCEs - two rated 10.0 and unauthenticated - trace back to three memory-corruption bugs in Onigmo, Ruby's regex engine, fixed in Ruby 4.0.9, 3.4.12 and 3.3.13. If your app builds a regex from user input (Regexp.new, or just /#{input}/), same boat. Ruby hasn't requested CVEs for these, so your scanner won't say a word.
