The uncomfortable thing about agents in production: your dashboards stay green while the agent quietly ignores half of what the user asked for. Google open-sourced AQuA, which reads live traces out of the request path, grades sessions against a nine-point checklist, then has a second model throw out findings it can't support — 3 of 9 clusters in their own eval. A 32-session sweep cost $3.76, and it never opens a PR by itself.
