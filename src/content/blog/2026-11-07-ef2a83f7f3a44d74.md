Nice unglamorous result on LLM graders: let a cheap model judge everything, escalate only the 30-90% confidence band to a frontier model. Hightouch hit 95.3% on their hard identity-resolution set vs 95.1% for Opus 5.5 alone, at $2.94 per 1,000 identities instead of $5.39. The real lesson is calibration, not price: the small model that won was wrong near 50% confidence, the one that lost was confidently wrong at 90%+.
