The best migrations are the boring ones. Yelp moved ~90 Cassandra clusters - about 1,000 pods and 1,000 TiB - onto EKS without streaming a byte between nodes. The insight: an EBS volume belongs to an Availability Zone, not a cluster, and a PersistentVolume is just a pointer to a volume ID. So they pre-created PVs against the existing IDs and let new pods claim them one at a time. Zero downtime, and rollback is the same operation in reverse.
