Out-of-band access for homelabs and small racks keeps getting cheaper. JetKVM Mini is a 42mm cube doing 1080p30 capture, USB keyboard and mouse, and virtual media from a TF card, streamed to a browser over WebRTC. It drops the Linux system for an ESP32-P4X with a hardware H.264 encoder, ships open-source firmware from launch, and starts at $39, or $33 in three-packs. Shipping October 26.
