Two years is a long time for a token to have the wrong owner. Converting a Docker Hub account to an organization re-pointed that user's personal access tokens to the new owner — without rotating the secret, and without asking them (CVE-2026-105445). Mint a PAT, name a target as owner, keep pushing as them. Docker reviewed ~304,000 pushes made with transferred tokens and found no abuse. Credential ownership isn't metadata.
