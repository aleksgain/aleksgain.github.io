Dan Luu's best argument isn't about speed, it's about what you let yourself attempt. His example is Michael Malis's pgrust - enough of a Postgres in Rust that testing it surfaced real bugs in Postgres itself, an SP-GiST numerical bug and a money overflow included. Luu reckons work that would have taken him a year now takes single-digit hours. The caveat he's honest about: it only holds where tests or benchmarks can tell you you're wrong.
