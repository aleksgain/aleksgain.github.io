52 security fixes in one PostgreSQL release, 32 of them at CVSS 8.8, and the headline bug traces back to a change made on 5 May 2004. CVE-2026-6460 lets anyone who can create objects run code as the Postgres OS user. Patched in 18.7, 17.12, 16.16, 15.20 and 14.25 — and rebuild your C extensions while you're there, or the fix may not actually cover you. 14.25 is also the last 14 ever.
