$1.2M in API spend, 948 working Linux privilege-escalation exploits. OpenAI's agentic pipeline chewed through 2,308 candidate kernel findings in mid-October, reproduced 1,724 of them inside 30 hours, and turned 948 into working LPEs plus 291 info leaks. Their own takeaway: treat an unpatched low-privilege account as functionally equivalent to root. Local privesc has been the "we'll get to it" bucket for years — that era looks over.
