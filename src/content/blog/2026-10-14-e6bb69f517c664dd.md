A $55 Android TV box is not a GPU, but 15 of them ran a 26B model at 0.39 tokens/s — and that is rather the point. Anthropic's Frontier Red Team got GLM-5.2 to replicate end-to-end across an 8-node emulated network in 4 of 5 trials, and inherited notes lifted a 9B model's inference-setup success from 45% to 84%. No worm in the wild, but "signs of life" with a doubling time of days is a planning horizon, not a headline.
