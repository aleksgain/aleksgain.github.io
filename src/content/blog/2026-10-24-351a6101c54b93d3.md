Good reminder that "load it all into a database first" is often the wrong reflex. CSIRO's sBeacon leaves VCF files sitting in S3 and queries them in place with Lambda and Athena, pulling only the regions it needs via HTTP byte-range requests. On chromosome 1 of 1000 Genomes (2,504 samples, 1.1GB compressed): 18s to onboard, 1.52s average query, about $0.00013 of compute. The headline's 100x claim never gets a baseline, mind you.
