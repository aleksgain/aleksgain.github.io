A copy loop from November 2010 is still shipping in your servers' firmware. CVE-2026-97631 is a buffer overflow in EDK II's PXE boot code: incoming ICMP messages get copied into a fixed 504-byte buffer with broken size checks on both the IPv4 and IPv6 paths. TianoCore rates it 9.5. The fix hit main on October 15, but no stable EDK II release carries it and RHEL 8/9/10 are still listed unfixed. Not network-booting? Turn PXE off.
