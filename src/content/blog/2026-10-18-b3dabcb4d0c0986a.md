The scary part here isn't a CVE - there isn't one. One stolen OAuth credential for ConnectWise Cloud Backup gave attackers mail, OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams access in 173 Microsoft 365 tenants, using permissions those tenants had already consented to. Huntress saw the tokens used on Oct 15, hitting tenants in sorted GUID order. And revoking user sessions won't save you: issued tokens stay valid up to 90 minutes after the app is disabled.
