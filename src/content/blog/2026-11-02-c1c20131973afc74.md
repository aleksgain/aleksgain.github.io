Here's a nasty one: LXD and MicroCloud checked every certificate a client presented against the trust store, so you could staple a trusted client's public cert behind your own and be accepted as them — no private key required. CVSS 10.0, and public certs were never meant to be secret. Fixed in LXD 6.10.1/5.21.9/5.0.11/4.0.15 and MicroCloud 3.4.1/2.1.5. Not exposed by default unless you set core.https_address — but MicroCloud clusters are.
