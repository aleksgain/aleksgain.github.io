Capacity planning just stopped being a formality. Andy Jassy says AWS's big committed capacity is largely sold out through 2027, with a chunk of 2028 already allocated — against roughly $220B of planned 2026 capex. Fair pushback from analysts: that's hyperscale pre-reserved blocks, not your day-to-day on-demand pool. But Capacity Blocks for ML already went up ~15% in January. If your 2027 plan assumes GPUs will just be there, go check.
