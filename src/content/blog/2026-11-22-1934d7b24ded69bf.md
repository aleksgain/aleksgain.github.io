Eight hundred developers locked out of production deploys, and the bug lived in three places at once. Intermittent timeouts to kube-apiserver over Private Service Connect were packet reordering in PSC, plus Envoy half-closing sockets so tcp_fin_timeout never started, plus a Linux 6.1 regression where tcp_ack() silently discarded a valid FIN. Fixed upstream in 6.9.7, shipped in GKE 1.33.5. Simultaneous pcaps at three layers cracked it.
