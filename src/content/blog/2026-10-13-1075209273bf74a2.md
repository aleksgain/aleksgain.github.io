Calendar entry for February 10, 2027: Let's Encrypt starts issuing 64-day certificates by default, on the way to 45 days in 2028. If your client speaks ACME Renewal Info, you can ignore this entirely. If renewal is a cron job with a hardcoded offset from years ago, Let's Encrypt's advice is to move it to about two-thirds of the certificate lifetime. The last 90-day cert expires May 11, 2027.
