Same model, same tasks, up to 5x the bill - purely from the harness you run it in. Runta burned 83B tokens over 27,280 agent runs across 18 harnesses and 5 open models: with the model fixed, cost per task swings 1.8x to 5.0x. The number I'd put on a slide is 64.5% of spend going to runs that failed. And rank doesn't transfer: Codex CLI was cheapest with GLM-5.3, 16th of 22 with Kimi-K3.
