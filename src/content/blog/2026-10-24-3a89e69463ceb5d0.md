Pillow sits in roughly every Python image pipeline - about 436 million downloads a month - and the nasty one here isn't in the image at all. CVE-2026-112900 (9.2) is argument injection through the EPS *filename*: Pillow hands it to Ghostscript, which reads it as instructions. Affects 2.6.0 up to 12.3.0, fixed in 13.0.0. If you store uploads under user-supplied names, that's you. Heads-up: 13.0.0 drops Python 3.10.
