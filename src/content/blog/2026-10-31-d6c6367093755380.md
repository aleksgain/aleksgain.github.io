Eleven hours in a marketplace was enough. On 29 October a tampered copy of the Claude Code extension, versions 2.1.312 and 2.1.313, was served from the Cursor marketplace. It appended an attacker SSH key labelled "svc-telemetry" to authorized_keys and persisted as "com.apple.sysmond" or a "SysMon" scheduled task. Anthropic's own systems were never touched. The marketplace was the attack surface, and auto-update did the clicking.
