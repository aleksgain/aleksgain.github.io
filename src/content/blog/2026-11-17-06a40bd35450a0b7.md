Yesterday your CSP either earned its keep or it didn't. Someone wrote malicious JavaScript straight into Optimizely Web customers' Project JavaScript field, so it shipped from cdn.optimizely.com with Optimizely's own cert: 188 infected snippets across 178 organisations, including the BBC, Zoom, CNN, Salesforce and GitLab. Of 256 affected hosts Report URI checked, 68 had no CSP at all and only 24 blocked the payload.
