800,000+ lines of production code from TypeScript to Rust in ~14.5 weeks and 128 PRs, with AI agents writing most of the implementation. The convincing part isn't the speed, it's the method: one component at a time behind an N-API shim so the existing end-to-end tests covered the new code. Startup through a single turn went from 5.25s to 292ms in-process. Read it before your next "let's just rewrite it" meeting.
