CVSS 10.0, unauthenticated RCE, and an affected range that starts at GitLab 7.0 — an integer overflow in the regex compiler that sat there for about twelve years. CVE-2026-110264 is fixed in 19.5.1, 19.4.3 and 19.3.6, and backported as far as 18.11.13. GitLab.com and Dedicated are already patched; your self-managed instance is not. They also shipped two detections, which helps if you can't take the downtime today.
