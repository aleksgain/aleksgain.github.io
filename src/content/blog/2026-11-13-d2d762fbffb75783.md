Ten years and five failed stabilisation attempts later, Rust's never type is stable in 1.100 — you can write Result<T, !> and mean it. Mind the breaking change: in editions before 2024, never-type fallback moves from () to !. The core Allocator trait landed too, and Cargo now does dependency cooldowns (global-min-publish-age = "3 days"), which is a quietly excellent defence against a freshly published malicious crate.
