A root daemon listening on a world-writable socket, with no check on who connects — that's CVE-2026-123039 in Check Point's Gaia OS, and it has been chained with PHP2Shell since late October. A couple of dozen customers got hit, ending in webshells, the TSGA backdoor and a wiper on a VMware ESXi host. Emergency hotfixes are out; if you can't patch tonight, stop dynamic_server.
