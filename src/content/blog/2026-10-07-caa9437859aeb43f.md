473.3 billion Git events a month, up 2.17x year over year, and 7.38 billion commits in September alone — more than five times last year. That's what pushed GitHub to decouple durability from compute: authoritative data in Azure Blob Storage, stateless workers serving reads, compaction and GC on their own fleet, up to 35x write throughput in internal benchmarks. Agents don't just write more code, they change the shape of the load.
