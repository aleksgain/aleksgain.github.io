Every control worked roughly as designed, and the chain still landed. NebuSec had Meta Muse export an attacker-controlled page to PDF, got code execution in the agent's container, walked conversation history and stored memories out past Sentinel with no approval prompt, then used generated kernel exploits to escape and take root of the user's VM — credential service included. Their exploit generator worked in over 99.9% of runs.
