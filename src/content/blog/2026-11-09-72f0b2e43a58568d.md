Broadcom's licensing turned "what do we do with the vSphere estate" into a board-level question, and the hyperscalers are now happy to sell you either answer. Google Cloud just GA'd self-managed VCF 9.x on bare metal in 22 regions: root on ESXi, vCenter and NSX, your own portable VCF subscriptions, nodes with 96 cores, 1.5 TB of RAM and 72 TB of local NVMe. You keep the keys — and the patching.
