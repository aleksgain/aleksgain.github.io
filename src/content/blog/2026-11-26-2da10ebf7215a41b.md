The alarming part of the 1Password incident isn't the vaults — those were never touched. It's the path: one employee's corporate GitHub account let the attacker push code into backend services and reach the CI/CD system holding infrastructure secrets. Names, emails and account IDs went out the door. They've also bumped invitation and recovery codes from 6 digits to 10 alphanumeric characters, which tells you what they were worried about.
