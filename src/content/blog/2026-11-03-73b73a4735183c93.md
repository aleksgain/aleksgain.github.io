Your patch backlog is mostly noise, and now there are numbers for it. Of the ~383,000 CVEs published since 1999, Microsoft counts 2,056 ever used in a real attack — fewer than 1 in 200. The ones that are used skew hard to takeover: server-side RCE goes from 20% of published CVEs to 37% of exploited ones, while XSS drops from 15% to 4%. And CISA's median publication-to-KEV time is now 3 days.
