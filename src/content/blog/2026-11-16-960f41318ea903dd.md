The Cursor breach has a lesson that isn't about Cursor: agent traces are secrets. One access key issued to Fireworks AI leaked, the coding-agent traces in a shared bucket held unredacted credentials, and those were used to pivot into SaaS tools, archived repos and support data. Result: a trojanized Claude Code extension (2.1.312/2.1.313) served from Cursor's marketplace cache for ~11 hours on 29 October, reaching up to 5,000 developers.
