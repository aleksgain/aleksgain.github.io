A patch on 21 October, a public PoC, then a real intrusion. That is the whole timeline for CVE-2026-76466 in Cisco Expressway, an unauthenticated root RCE at CVSS 9.8. Russian firm F6 watched attackers take two internet-facing nodes, pivot to two more cluster members, then reach GitLab repos and pull out credentials. Four devices from one unpatched edge box. Treat the PoC date as your patch deadline, not the advisory date.
