More than 13,000 malicious GitHub repos pushed in 34 hours, peaking at 2,999 an hour. FakeGit is back with 17,610 repos pushing SmartLoader and StealC behind fake README download buttons, and Apiiro found 71% of them missing from URLhaus before the report. Roughly 800 posed as AI skills or MCP servers in public registries, which is the part I'd worry about most.
