Your dev server was never meant to face the internet, and attackers know it. F5 logged over 800 attacks and roughly 32,000 events in a single month against exposed Vite servers, appending things like ?raw to slip past the file filter (CVE-2026-39364, Vite 7.1.0-7.3.2 and 8.x before 8.0.5). They go straight for .env files, AWS creds, Azure tokens and Terraform state. Patch, close port 5173, rotate anything that was reachable 😅
