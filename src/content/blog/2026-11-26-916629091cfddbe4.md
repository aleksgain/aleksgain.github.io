Hex.pm — where every Elixir and Erlang build pulls its dependencies — just took a CVSS 10.0: an unauthenticated POST could run OS commands, because a safety check was deleted in February 2016 and nobody noticed for nearly 11 years. Six production servers were hit by 69 requests in 13 minutes, and the fix was live 15 minutes after the first one. No packages were altered, but go change your password and reset 2FA.
