Netflix says a typical application regression investigation takes about four hours, and most of that is spent reconstructing how the systems relate to each other. So they built an ontology: incidents, deployments, alerts and dependencies as RDF triples in QuipuDB, with a correlation engine over 13 signal types feeding ranked hypotheses to their AutoRCA agents. No outcome numbers yet, but "the graph is the interface" beats another dashboard.
