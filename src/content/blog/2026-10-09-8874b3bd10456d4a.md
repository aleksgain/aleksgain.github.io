Kafka without the cluster: Cloudflare's K2 is a partitioned durable log built straight on R2, with no separate coordination service because R2's atomic operations handle ordering and offsets. Public beta is free, capped at 30 MB/s produce and 10 GB per stream. Cloudflare cites ~1s p99 produce latency, but one beta user measured p99 end-to-end at 7.5s. Read the tail numbers before you plan around it.
