Found an interesting one for anyone who still opens a disassembler. REA is an MCP server that gives Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or Gemini CLI a real analysis engine - Ghidra, IDA Pro or Hopper - plus static passes over Electron, .NET and Android. Its DX-Ball example rebuilt a sound positioning routine that passed 3,205 tests against the original x86 and matched all 63 compiled bytes. Analysis is local; your findings still go to the model provider.
