If you're on Next.js 16 with Cache Components, this is your afternoon: CVSS 9.5, remote code execution, no known workaround. The advisory covers 16.0.0 through 16.4.0 and the trigger is a `use cache` function returning multiple typed arrays - fixed in 16.4.1. Vercel recommends upgrading even if your app has no such function, which tells you roughly how much fun auditing that by hand would be 😅
