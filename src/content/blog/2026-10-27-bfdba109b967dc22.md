Cloudflare just published the kind of AI postmortem I wish were standard. For two weeks in late August, 155 GLM-5.3 responses on Workers AI could contain text from other customers' prompts. The cause: a 1,048,576-token context window sitting on a prefill KV cache pool of only 681,280 tokens, plus an SGLang bug where over-length requests were marked rejected but never aborted - so decode attended over stale KV pages. Anyone self-hosting: v0.5.22+.
