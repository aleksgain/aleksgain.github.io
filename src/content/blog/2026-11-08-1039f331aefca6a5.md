M3 support in Asahi Linux just left EXPERT-flag territory, and the team says early adopters reported almost nothing. DCP is enabled now, which buys working sleep, better battery life and a usable notch area. Thunderbolt does USB3, XDomain and networking downstream — PCIe and DisplayPort tunnelling, i.e. most docks, still don't. And the AVD video decode driver went upstream at roughly 13,000 lines after three months downstream.
