The honest number from GitHub's AI security pilot: 9,791 findings in, 99 reports out - about one per hundred survived triage plus manual review by seven researchers spending two days per project. 55 are fixed, 14 shipped in a release. Netty's maintainers nailed it: "Finding vulnerabilities is no longer our bottleneck. Fixing them is the hard part." So if you point a scanner at someone's repo, bring the patch too.
