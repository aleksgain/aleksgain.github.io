Discord is throwing out Electron and React Native and rebuilding both clients in Rust on GPUI, the GPU-accelerated framework from the Zed editor team. Early internal builds: ~290 MB RAM with two accounts signed in, and a near-instant cold start against 5.5 seconds today. The part I would flag - GPUI hands you no text layout, no IME integration, no screen-reader hooks and no RTL. Accessibility stops being Chromium's problem and becomes yours.
