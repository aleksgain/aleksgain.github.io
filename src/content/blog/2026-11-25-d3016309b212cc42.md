The merge gate is the interesting part. Chainguard runs agent fleets across ~3,000 image repos, and stopped trusting "CI said it passed": merges now require an attestation binding commit SHA, environment and test verdict, and the harness runs the validators, not the model. Package bumps auto-merge ~99% of the time; CVE remediation lands 97-98% weekly. The honest bit: sealed default-deny microVMs made odd test failures far harder to debug.
