Speech to text in 16.9 MB, one file, CPU only, no dependencies. Whistle beats Whisper base on LibriSpeech, SPGISpeech and Earnings-22 at about 8.6x smaller, and hits first token in 11.1 ms on an M4 Pro versus 73.2 ms, partly because Whisper pads every clip to 30 seconds. Whisper base still wins on TED-LIUM and AMI, so check your domain, but on-device transcription just got a lot cheaper.
