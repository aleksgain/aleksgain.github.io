Netflix runs millions of containers a week on Titus and has now added a mode that runs exactly one task per EC2 instance. Not for security — for noisy neighbours. cgroups and seccomp cannot partition last-level cache or memory bandwidth, and the HTB traffic shaper takes a global lock, so one container spraying small packets can starve the host. They skipped Firecracker and Kata because nested virtualization was not ready on their instance types.
