OpenTofu 1.13 goes after one of my least favourite planning errors: a count that depends on a value unknown until apply. New convert and assumenotnull functions let you give an unknown yamldecode result a known object type, so count can resolve to 0 or 1 while the object's contents stay unknown - no two-stage apply. Also in: experimental linting behind -lint=all, and WinRM is removed from remote-exec. 1.13.x is supported until 1 August 2027.
