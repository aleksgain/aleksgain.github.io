The prompt-to-app era has a default-open problem. Researchers crawled 159,041 Lovable projects; of the 34,545 wired up to Supabase, 24,664 handed over database rows to anyone who merely loaded the page — no login, no interaction. One German forum leaked 80,000 profiles and more than 200,000 chat messages; another app exposed 1,000+ records with passport numbers and expiry dates. Access control is the one thing you can't vibe your way past.
