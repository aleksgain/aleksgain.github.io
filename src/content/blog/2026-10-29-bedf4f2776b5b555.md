Octoverse 2026 has the number I'll be quoting all year: by September, more than 40% of pull requests on GitHub were agent-started — 110 million in that month alone, roughly six times March's 17%. They also land slightly faster (90% merged within two weeks) and touch more files (87% multi-file vs 79%). Commits hit 34.3 billion for the year, up from 12.9B. Authoring clearly isn't the bottleneck anymore; review is.
