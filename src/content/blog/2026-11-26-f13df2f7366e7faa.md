Someone pointed Lean 4 at Clojure 1.12.7 and came back with 17 bugs they couldn't find in JIRA. My favourite: reduce on a vector-of never returns once you pass 65,505 elements, because a 0xffe0 mask quietly clears bit 16 and up. Meanwhile 3 million differential ops against java.util found zero hash-map mismatches — so the data structures hold, it's the arithmetic edges that leak. Formal methods as a bug-finding tool, not a research project.
