Anonymous access is on by default in Red Hat Quay, and that detail turns out to matter a lot. CVE-2026-123710 (CVSS 9.8) lets anyone who can reach the registry read and overwrite private images in every repository, then plant a non-expiring signing key that survives restarts. A second flaw at 9.9 leaks other tenants' robot tokens, because an SQL LIKE pattern never escaped underscores. Every Quay 3 and 5 version is affected.
