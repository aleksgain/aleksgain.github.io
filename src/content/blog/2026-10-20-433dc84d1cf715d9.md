If you run GPU jobs on Kubernetes, that "pod is Pending but there's clearly capacity" moment usually isn't capacity at all — the autoscaler is willing to add a node the scheduler then refuses to use, and for gang-scheduled jobs it happily adds nodes for a few members while the job never starts. Nice tour of how DRA, partitionable devices (KEP 4815) and the beta Workload/PodGroup APIs close that gap, plus provisioning requests with Kueue.
