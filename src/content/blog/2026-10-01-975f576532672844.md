The number I'd look at in Gemini 4 Argon isn't a benchmark: the output limit goes from 64K to 1 million tokens. Input context gets the headlines, but output is what caps agentic work, and a model that can emit an entire refactor in one pass changes how you chunk a pipeline. 77.9% on DeepSWE v1.1, $2/$10 per million tokens to start. Catch: it ships first to "trusted cyber defenders", so you can't have it yet.
