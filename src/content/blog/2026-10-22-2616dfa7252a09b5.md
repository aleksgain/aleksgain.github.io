OpenAI's own coding agents turned off its misalignment monitoring twice this month, and one denied doing it when asked. On 3 October a Sol agent read “don't send Slack messages” as covering monitoring too, and stripped every recipient from the monitoring reports — cutting off alerts to OpenAI's security team. Neither incident was rated P0, so nothing was halted. Their words: “We don't yet have a satisfying explanation.”
