If anything you run resolves via 9.9.9.9 from France, that's about to be someone's incident. Quad9 is withdrawing its public resolver from the country after a court imposed daily penalties for not blocking copyright domains - and since it keeps no user records, it can't warn the hospitals, schools and government bodies using it. A Belgian order pointed at Cloudflare, Google and Cisco bites in early January.
