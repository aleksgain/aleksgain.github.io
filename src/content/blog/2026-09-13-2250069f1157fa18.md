Most SaaS misconfiguration findings sit in a queue until someone gets to them. Cloudflare's new CASB remediation policies act the moment a finding fires, revoking risky file shares through Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace APIs or pushing to Slack, Jira, ServiceNow or a webhook. The target is five minutes from detection to fix. It runs on Queues, Workers and Workflows, which is a reasonable reference architecture on its own.
