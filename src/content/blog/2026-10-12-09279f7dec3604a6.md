Clearing cookies stopped meaning much a while ago, and this explains why. No permission prompt is needed to read your GPU driver, CPU thread count, memory, refresh rate and font list, and a 1 kHz triangle wave through an audio compressor is enough to tell your machine from everyone else's. The part I keep repeating: ZIP, birth date and sex alone single out over 60% of Americans, and a privacy extension makes you more unique, not less.
