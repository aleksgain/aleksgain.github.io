25 million CI checkouts a day, which fetched naively would be over 600 PB of git traffic — that's the number that made Anthropic write its own VCS instead of babysitting a few hundred git mirrors. Scion keeps changesets in a write-ahead log and serves only the trees you ask for: checkouts went from minutes to seconds, at ~200x less CPU and memory. Seven people, five months. No public release planned 😅
