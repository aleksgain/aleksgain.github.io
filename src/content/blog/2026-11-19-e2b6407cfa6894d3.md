Buried in Google's Gemini 4 Neon launch is the number I actually care about: on SWE-sweep, the same model scores 12.8% under the mini-SWE harness and 39.0% inside Google Antigravity. Triple, from the scaffolding alone. The rest is solid - $3/M in, $15/M out, 1M context, 32% lower cost per task than Carbon at high thinking. But file that first number away for the next time someone shows you a model benchmark with no harness attached.
