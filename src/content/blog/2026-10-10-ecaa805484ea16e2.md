Google just paused its open-source bug bounty over AI-generated spam; Anthropic's answer is to point the models at the code instead. OSS Scanner is free and opt-in — maintainers enroll with a PR and a Dockerfile. Six months of internal runs produced 29,000+ candidate findings, and in one sample 85 of 97 critical/high reports (88%) met the disclosure bar. Reports ship unreviewed, so maintainers absorb the triage.
