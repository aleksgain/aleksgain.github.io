If you've ever flinched at a Mapbox invoice, this is worth ten minutes. The whole planet as one 139GB PMTiles file on object storage, served straight over HTTP range requests — no tile server, no containers to babysit. Storage with 5x replication lands near $5/month, a city-level browsing session moves about 5MB, and the author works that out to roughly $0.05 per 1k map loads against Mapbox's $5. Clip to a bounding box and it shrinks a lot.
