The headline doing the rounds says someone decompiled the Adobe suite and open-sourced it in Rust. The repo says something different: PhotoCraft is a clean-room build from public specs and observed behaviour, MIT/Apache-2.0, with PSD support written against Adobe's published spec. 19.4k stars already, and the README is refreshingly honest - early alpha, ~20 tools still missing, no plug-in compatibility.
