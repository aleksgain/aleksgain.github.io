Spanner without the atomic clocks. Spanner Omni is GA and runs on AWS, on-prem, or a laptop: TrueTime becomes software-based error-bounded time sync, and Colossus becomes a layer over local disks that Google itself describes as "not Colossus, but a sufficient stand-in". They claim millions of queries per second across petabytes in one regional deployment — and offer no availability SLA, which tells you who is holding the pager now.
