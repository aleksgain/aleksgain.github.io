A deadline worth a calendar entry: on October 11 the DNS root stops being signed by KSK-2017 and moves to KSK-2024, key tag 38696. Same RSA/SHA-256, no algorithm change, and if your validating resolver does RFC 5011 it has had the key since January 2025. The ones that break are the resolvers where someone pinned a trust anchor by hand years ago and never revisited it. dnstest.dev/ksk-2024 answers that in a second.
