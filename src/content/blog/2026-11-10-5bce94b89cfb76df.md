Reminder that "safe Rust" is a contract the standard library also has to hold up its end of. Microsoft's FORGE Lab, partly using an AI scanner, reported soundness bugs in std whose proofs of concept are pure safe Rust: a panic inside your own Clone impl could drop uninitialized memory, and process::exit from a child thread unmapped the main thread's alternate signal stack — a bug sitting there since 2015. Nearly 11 years 😅
