42 hours on a 512 GB box versus 1 hour 40 minutes on 128 GB — that's the number Tiger Data leads with for pg_vectorsearch, its new Postgres extension. Instead of HNSW's graph, the PRISM index clusters similar vectors into compressed, densely packed partitions and scans them sequentially. It reads your existing pgvector columns, so trying it is cheap. Still alpha, though: PG18+, 1968 dimensions max, and filtered WHERE + ORDER BY waits for GA.
