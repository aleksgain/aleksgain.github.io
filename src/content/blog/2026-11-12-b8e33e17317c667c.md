Fire suppression as the outage. Contractors made dust next to Jane Street's only NYC datacenter, the smoke alarm tripped, and that alarm was wired to shut down the CRAC units. Temperatures logged 141, 142, 143, 143.5, 143.6F while someone ran nine minutes and rode 33 floors up to pull power cords by hand. What saved it: Hong Kong noticed many servers warming at once. One hot box is normal, a whole fleet drifting up is a cooling failure.
