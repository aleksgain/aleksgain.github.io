Bearer tokens were always a bad idea we all shipped anyway, so it's good to see Google adding DPoP to its OAuth platform: tokens cryptographically bound to a client-generated key pair, useless to a thief without the private key. Note the scope — refresh tokens only, which Google calls "the primary vector for persistent online impersonation." Access tokens stay plain Bearer, because asymmetric verification on every request costs too much.
