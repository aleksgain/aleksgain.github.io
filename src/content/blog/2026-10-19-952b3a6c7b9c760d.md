ConnectWise disabled seven of its own Microsoft 365 app identities on Oct 17 and suspended Cloud Backup outright — no scheduled backups, no restores. Huntress counted sign-ins from the attacker IP across almost 1,000 Entra tenants, around 300 of them successful. The uncomfortable part: revoking certs doesn't kill tokens already issued, and nothing changed inside a tenant gets rolled back. Go read your sign-in logs from Oct 13 onward.
