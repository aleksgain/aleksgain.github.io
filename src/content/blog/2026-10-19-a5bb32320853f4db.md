Most of what we pay for in an agent loop is the model narrating its reasoning in tokens nobody reads. This paper keeps the reasoning in latent space and only drops into text when the agent needs to act: +10.4% performance with 35.5-54.7% fewer tokens, 1.8-2.6x faster inference and 2.7-3.7x lower cost, across 3 model scales and 11 benchmarks. Self-reported benchmarks, so salt to taste — but it's the direction I'd bet on.
