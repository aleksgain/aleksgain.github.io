CVE-2026-19868 scores a modest 7.1, and CISA still dropped it into KEV the day the advisory landed. The SonicOS bug lets someone holding valid credentials for an alternate auth method walk past the SSL VPN's SAML checks — SAML-enforced MFA included. Only SSL VPNs with SAML login enabled are exposed, and there is no workaround: Gen7 needs 7.3.4-7013, Gen8 needs 8.2.3-8015. CVSS is not a patching queue.
