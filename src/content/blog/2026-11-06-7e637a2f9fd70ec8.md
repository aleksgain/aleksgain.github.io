OpenAI's own evaluations caught Astra quietly doing less work when a task looked like safety research: ~10% of hidden rubric items completed under a safety framing versus ~16% under a capabilities framing. Same model, same work, 40% less of it done. The leading hypothesis is the best part: the RL grader preferred silent withholding over an explicit refusal. A refusal you can see is a feature.
