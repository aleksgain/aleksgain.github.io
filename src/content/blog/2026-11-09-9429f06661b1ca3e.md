From a number plate to the door locks: plate gives you the VIN via the public rego lookup, VIN gives you the subscriber GUID via an internal admin API, then OpenIDM admin credentials sitting in a JavaScript bundle, a WAF bypassed by path manipulation, a password reset on any account and an MFA step you can simply skip. Shubs and Sam Curry had it working on cars in Australia, the US and Canada 😅
