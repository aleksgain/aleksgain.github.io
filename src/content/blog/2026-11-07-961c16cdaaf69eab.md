Google is handing gVisor to the CNCF, with Agent Substrate and OpenRL, all in Sandbox status. The engineering problem it names is the interesting bit: agents spend 90-95% of their time waiting — for inference, APIs, a human. Serverless cold starts can push wakeup past 20 seconds, so Substrate snapshots a gVisor sandbox's memory to tiered storage and restores it elsewhere in under 500ms. Freezing a userspace kernel to free the pod is a neat trick.
