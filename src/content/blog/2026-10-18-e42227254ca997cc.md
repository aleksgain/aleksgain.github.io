The interesting line isn't the acquisition, it's AWS's framing: 90%+ of queries run on about 1TB of data or less, which is exactly DuckDB's territory. Aurora PostgreSQL is the first AWS service to use it, so one query can join live transactional rows with Iceberg and Parquet in S3 - no reverse ETL, no extra charge. MIT licence and the DuckDB Foundation stay put, which is the part the community will be watching.
