Mistral just dropped a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal MoE on Hugging Face under a modified MIT license. The number I keep rereading isn't the parameter count: 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, level with GLM-5.3 while using 47% fewer tokens per task. Terminal-Bench 4.0 sits at 41%, up 13 points from the preview with 30% fewer output tokens. Running a MaaS on it still needs a separate commercial license.
