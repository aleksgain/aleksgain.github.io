The uncomfortable part isn't that an unreleased Anthropic model found bugs — it's that it chained them into working exploits. Four flaws (V8, a sandbox escape, a CacheStorage race, a FedCM use-after-free) became one-click RCE on stock Chrome for macOS. Google assigned 62 CVEs, all critical or high, 13 of them sandbox escapes. The Linux chain: 48 minutes to leak a kernel pointer, five more to a root shell.
