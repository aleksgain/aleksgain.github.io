Rare take on the npm-versus-platform argument that doesn't scold anyone. Lawson's example is a modal: positioning, scroll locking, a focus trap, Escape handling, all of which native <dialog> hands you for free. His own team once built custom compression on top of ClickHouse, which compresses automatically. With Safari now shipping ~7 releases a year, "the platform is too lumpy" is expiring as an excuse. Mostly we just like building things.
