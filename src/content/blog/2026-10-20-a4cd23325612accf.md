An 11-hour window is all it takes. Rank Math PRO downloads from the vendor's own site between Oct 15 20:02 UTC and Oct 16 07:12 UTC shipped v3.0.123 with a backdoor that recreates itself whenever someone visits the site and phones home to rank1cdn[.]com. The part people will get wrong: updating or deactivating does not remove it. If you activated that build, treat the host as compromised and rotate every key and salt. v3.0.124 is the fix.
