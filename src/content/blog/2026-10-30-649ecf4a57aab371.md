A use-after-free in IPv4 TCP connection setup, in the kernel since 4.4, and nobody agrees how bad it is: 9.8 from the kernel CVE team, 8.1 from Oracle and SUSE, 5.9 "Moderate" from Red Hat. CVE-2026-98432 is fixed upstream and shipped by Debian, Oracle Linux and SUSE — but Ubuntu 22.04/24.04/26.04 are "work in progress" and RHEL 8/9/10 are "deferred". Worth tracking, not filing away.
