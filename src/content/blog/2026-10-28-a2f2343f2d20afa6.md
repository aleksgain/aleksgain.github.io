Linux kernel CVEs went from about two a week to roughly 16 a day, with 848 published on a single day in August. AWS's pipeline: a plain filter that drops ~70% as not-applicable in their config, then an agent that classifies each remaining CVE in 3.4 seconds instead of minutes, then two engineers. The honest part is the error bar - roughly 5-10% of the agent's calls are judged problematic, which is exactly why humans stay in the loop.
