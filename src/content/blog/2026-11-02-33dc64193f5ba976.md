A release slipping because of too many bug reports is a new kind of problem. Robert Haas says Postgres 19 will probably land in November rather than mid-September, and points at LLMs: back-patched commits to the stable branch went from 724 to 1,169 year over year, up over 60%, and three features were reverted in three weeks. The bugs were always there in a 30-year-old codebase. Finding them is now cheap; reviewing the fixes isn't.
