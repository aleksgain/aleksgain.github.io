Someone shipped a Linux kernel module that unlocks the BF16/FP16 instruction issue rate on Ampere GeForce cards to their professional equivalents. On a 3090 Ti at 450W, dense 8192 cubed GEMM goes from 87.50 to 157.41 TFLOPS in BF16, and TF32 from 42.58 to 82.61. Before you rebuild your initramfs: LLM prefill gains 14-36%, decode and INT8 barely move. The segmentation was in the issue rate all along.
