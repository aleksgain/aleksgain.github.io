"Why is my Job still pending?" has two completely different answers once Kueue is in the picture: the scheduler couldn't place a Pod, or Kueue never admitted the Workload. A new read-only Headlamp plugin (0.1.0-alpha) walks that whole admission path. The worked example is the classic one - a ClusterQueue with 2 CPUs of quota, a Job asking for 3, QuotaReserved=False, nothing happens. Beats grepping through YAML at 2am.
