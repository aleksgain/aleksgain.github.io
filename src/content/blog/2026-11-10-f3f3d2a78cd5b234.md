That vendor integration role nobody owns anymore? Go look at it today. Wiz tracked an actor assuming Qubole's cross-account roles in customer AWS accounts from 14-26 October — every AssumeRole carried the right External ID and succeeded, then went for Lambda env vars, Secrets Manager and SSM. External IDs sit in plaintext in CloudTrail, so they were never secrets. Wiz says an aws:SourceIp condition would have blocked every one.
