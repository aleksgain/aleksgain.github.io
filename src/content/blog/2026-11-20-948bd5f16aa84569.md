Buried in Cloudflare's 2026 midterms report: the DDoS story everyone braces for didn't happen — zero significant attacks against ~1,400 election-related sites on Election Day. The quieter number is the interesting one. Vulnerability exploit attempts on government election sites ran 180x more often than DDoS, climbing from 200k a day in January to 2 million a day in October, 392 million mitigated over 12 months. Attackers want in, not down.
