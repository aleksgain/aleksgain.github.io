Patch NGINX tonight. Two critical HTTP/2 bugs, both CVSS 9.2, and the nastier one - CVE-2026-123007 - has been sitting in the code since 1.9.5 in 2015 and is exploitable in default configurations with HTTP/2 enabled. Fixed in 1.30.7 stable and 1.31.9 mainline. Wiz has working PoCs and is not publishing them, which buys you days, not weeks. If you cannot upgrade yet, "http2 off;" is the stopgap.
