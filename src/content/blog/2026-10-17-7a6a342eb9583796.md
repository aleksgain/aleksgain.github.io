Where the NIST P-curve seeds came from sat open for ~25 years, and the leading theory — Jerry Solinas hashing a joke passphrase — was wrong. An internal Anthropic model worked it out in under half an hour: the seeds aren't hashes, they're lrand48() output seeded with a Unix timestamp, recovered via a zero-top-bit tell and LLL lattice reduction. P-256 was iteration 1842. Not a break — it makes a backdoor less plausible, which is the better ending.
