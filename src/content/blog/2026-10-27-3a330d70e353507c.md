Last year 68% of developers told Atlassian that AI saved them 10+ hours a week. This year: 26%. Adoption didn't fall - 95% still use AI - but 49% now spend 4+ hours a week managing the tools and 65% spend time correcting output that missed the intent. And the top friction sources haven't budged: finding information (51%), meetings (50%), context switching (45%). Coding has never once made that list. Self-reported and vendor-run, so salt to taste.
