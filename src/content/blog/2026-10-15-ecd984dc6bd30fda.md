$40,000 of compute bought a $250,000 bug bounty. That's what XBOW spent over six days, using a publicly available GPT-5.6 model, to chain three unknown Chrome bugs into code execution on Windows from a single page load, through both of Chrome's sandboxes. Google paid its top VRP reward. Their first full chain took two months; the researcher estimates four humans would have needed 6-9 months. The economics of exploitation just moved 😅
