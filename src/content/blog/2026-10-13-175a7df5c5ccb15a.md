Patch-tonight material if you run GitLab self-managed. CVE-2026-97229 is an integer overflow in the engine that compiles the regexes in your pipeline config, and GitLab says it could let an unauthenticated user run code on the server. Your CI config parser as the front door is a nasty shape. Fixed in 19.2.8, 19.3.4 and 19.4.2 — GitLab.com and Dedicated are already covered.
