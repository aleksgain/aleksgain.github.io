Patched Tuesday, exploited by Thursday. SonicWall's max-severity SMA1000 flaw (CVE-2026-102255) lets an unauthenticated attacker make the appliance issue requests for them, and honeypots already show OPTIONS requests probing the internal CouchDB at 127.0.0.1:5984 with admin:admin. Shadowserver counts 400+ exposed appliances. CISA has added 19 SonicWall bugs to KEV in four years — at some point the appliance is the threat model.
