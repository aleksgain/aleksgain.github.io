The interesting part of Arctic Wolf's AntiFox write-up isn't the AI, it's how ordinary the trail looks. Web shell on an IIS box, PowerShell hunting for web.config and connection strings, then a valid IAM identity enumerating dozens of services. Most of those calls came back AccessDenied. One GetSecretValue against a production secret didn't. Around 45 of these agentic frameworks sit on GitHub now. Your AccessDenied noise is a signal.
