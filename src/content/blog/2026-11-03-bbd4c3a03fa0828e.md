The scary number in this one isn't the AI part. Kernel 5.10.y is carrying 2,006 unfixed CVEs, and Debian 13 has 1,128 source packages with unfixed CVEs across 4,562 unique ones. Trail of Bits has now landed backports for 149 CVEs into stable kernel branches, with Codex adapting the patches and a human spending ~30 minutes on a straightforward one. Backporting is exactly the unglamorous work nobody was ever going to fund.
