Raft's weak spot was never the crash, it's the leader that's merely slow — on a global network you can burn a second noticing. Cloudflare's Meerkat control plane runs QuePaxa, where the leader is an optimisation, not a requirement: ~95% of decisions still land in one round trip, and killing the leader only sagged the decision rate to 65% for a minute. Nothing halted. Behind it: a 2020 switch failure left a dashboard 80x slower for six hours.
