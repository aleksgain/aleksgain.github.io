PostgreSQL 19 is out, and the headline for me isn't a new SQL toy — it's REPACK (CONCURRENTLY), which reclaims bloat and can reorder a table to an index without blocking reads or writes. That's VACUUM FULL's job minus the maintenance window. Also: pg_plan_advice finally gives Postgres a sanctioned way to pin a plan, autovacuum can vacuum indexes with parallel workers, and JIT is now off by default because its cost estimates proved unreliable.
