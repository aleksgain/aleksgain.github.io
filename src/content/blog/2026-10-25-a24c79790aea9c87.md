Google let Gemini 4 Argon loose on its Agent Development Kit for 11 hours unattended - 200,000+ lines, ~800 files, 16,892 unit tests - and kept two of the six changes it proposed. Session-prep overhead fell about 40%, cold package import time 21%. The other four were rejected on review for reliability or maintainability. That 2-of-6 ratio is the number I'd quote, not the 11 hours: the agent is the cheap part, the review isn't.
