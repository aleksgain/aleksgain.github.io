Seventeen CVEs in one hotfix, and attackers were using the public exploit chain hours after it appeared. N-able's N-central 2026.4 HF3 covers CVE-2026-129271 through -129287 and finally removes the legacy API toggle - "4EverAble2Shell" paired that toggle with an Apache Axis RCE for root, then dropped miners. The same Axis bug is being thrown at Confluence, so patch both. RMM boxes remain the best job in attacking.
