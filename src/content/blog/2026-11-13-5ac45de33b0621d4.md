Sometimes the answer to "can't we just use Argo CD?" is no. LinkedIn runs 250+ clusters, 4,000+ services and 100,000+ deployments a week, and Argo CD's one-watch-per-resource model meant etcd churn in one app slowed syncs for all of them — under 3 minutes at 60,000 pods, over 10 minutes past 80,000. Their replacement runs each deployment in its own ephemeral pod. Infra-caused deploy failures: 925 down to 63, against 3.7x the volume.
