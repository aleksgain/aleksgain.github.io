The release note said: "Enabled automatic background-server startup for eligible interactive sessions." What Codex CLI 0.157.0 actually does is leave a daemon running after you close every window, copy ~374 MB of itself into ~/.codex/packages, and start a detached updater that checks hourly and can restart the backend mid-turn. The opt-out exists — `codex features disable daemon_auto_start` — it's just missing from the config reference.
