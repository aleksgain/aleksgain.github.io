Project Zero is done negotiating. The old 90+30 model is gone, replaced by one flat deadline from the day a bug is reported: Full 90 now, Full 60 from 2 Feb 2027, Full 30 from 3 Jan 2028 — and whether you shipped a fix stops being a factor. Their reasoning is the uncomfortable bit: patches get reverse-engineered fast enough that soak time was only ever buying false secrecy. Your patch pipeline has 13 months to get twice as fast.
