On October 24 AWS will unplug its European Sovereign Cloud from the global backbone for several hours and let traffic fall back to the public internet over European ISP links. An EU-resident team in Brandenburg runs the region, on a local in-EU copy of the source code. Good resilience test — but as one practitioner put it, redundancy is not the same as independence, and nobody has said what IAM and DNS do while the region is cut off.
