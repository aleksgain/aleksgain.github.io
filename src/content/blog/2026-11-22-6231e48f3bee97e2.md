Eleven minutes from "what API is this?" to writing arbitrary files on the box. Amazon's threat intel team got a look inside a China-nexus operator's Cairn instance (left with no auth on it, naturally 😅) and watched autonomous agents map MONAI Label, find a path traversal bug nobody had reported, and work it into a root shell. A ~48-hour campaign against East Asian universities. The agents found the zero-day, the humans just pointed them.
