SHA-pinning Actions always felt like homework you did once and never again. GitHub's workflow lockfiles are now GA: actions.lock records commits for direct and transitive deps, the runner uses those even if the tag moves, and Dependabot updates the lockfile in the same PR. Which is the catch — auto-merge those PRs and you've automated trusting whatever moved. Also diarise: pull_request_target gets blocked by default on December 2.
