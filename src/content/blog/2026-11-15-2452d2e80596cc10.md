MongoDB-compatible, Postgres underneath, MIT licensed: DocumentDB just hit 1.0, now a Linux Foundation project with AWS, Google, Snowflake and Yugabyte behind it. It's a Postgres extension plus a protocol gateway, covers PG 15-18, and GA adds a Kubernetes operator. The licence is the real story — MIT against Mongo's SSPL means you can host it commercially without opening your own code. Benchmarks are still messy, so measure before you believe anyone.
