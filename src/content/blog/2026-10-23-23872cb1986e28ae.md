Third VeloCloud Orchestrator flaw Arista has flagged as exploited since late July, and the first of them that needs a login: CVE-2026-114625, CVSS 9.9, a SQL injection that takes an Enterprise Read Only account all the way to operator. On-prem orchestrators need 5.2.3.18, 6.1.3.10 or 6.4.2.12; hosted and dedicated are already patched. Arista published two attacker IPs but not when the attacks started.
