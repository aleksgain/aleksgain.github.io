Python Workers are GA, and the detail I'd look at isn't the headline — it's that the JavaScript glue is gone. Passing a dict to a queue used to mean to_js(..., dict_converter=js.Object.fromEntries); now it's just a dict. Cloudflare also pushed PEP 783 to standardize Wasm wheels through cibuildwheel, which matters more long term. Caveat: the ecosystem is still adopting it, so check your deps before planning a migration.
