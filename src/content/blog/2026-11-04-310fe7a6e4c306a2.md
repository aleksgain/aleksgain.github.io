Your cloud bill is now a detection surface. Google's threat intel team reports LLMjacking — stealing credentials to run someone else's inference on your account — passing $100,000 in fraudulent charges in a single day, and $1 million at some orgs before access was cut. The way in is boring: leaked Service Account keys, GitHub PATs, session tokens pasted into a fake portal that walks past MFA. Spend alerts are security tooling now.
