GitHub is moving Git writes from a per-repository lock to a per-reference quorum, and the stated reason is agents. When a developer and three agents push to four different branches, the old design made them all queue behind the slowest replica — about 25ms away in Azure. Per-reference quorums gave 3-4x higher write throughput at lower latency in internal testing. Buried in there: Spokes handles well over 100 billion operations per day.
