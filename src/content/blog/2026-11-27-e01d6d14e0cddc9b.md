If you patched Drupal on 18 November and moved on, go back. A second advisory — SA-CORE-2026-019, scored 21/25 and exploited in the wild — says sites left in unusual or incomplete install states were still open to full takeover, and you can't tell from the UI whether yours is one. Fixes are 11.4.14, 11.3.23 and 10.6.23, and you must run update.php for the hook that repairs existing sites.
