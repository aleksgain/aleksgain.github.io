If your app's transactional mail started bouncing off Outlook.com this week, here's why: since 5 November it rejects messages from domains with no DMARC record — "550 5.7.1 Message rejected: Message lacks SPF/DKIM/DMARC Auth Records" — and unlike the 2025 rule, this one isn't limited to senders above 5,000 a day. No announcement from Microsoft. A v=DMARC1; p=none TXT record is a five-minute fix; alignment is the part people get wrong.
