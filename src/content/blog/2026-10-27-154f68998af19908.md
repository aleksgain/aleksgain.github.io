$1 per million input tokens, $5 output - half what GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol cost, with OpenAI claiming 3x faster task completion. The number I'd actually plan around isn't the price: internal error rate at low reasoning effort fell from 53.6% to 16.7%, and Terminal-Bench 4.0 at max effort went from 21.5% to 50.3%. OpenAI still points you at Sol or Astra for the hardest coding work, which is a refreshingly un-marketed footnote.
