A systems language with no heap: every dynamic value lives inline on compiler-managed data stacks and is freed when its scope ends. No allocator, no GC, no refcounting, no lifetime annotations - and a reference into an array stays valid as the array grows. Across 16 benchmarks Goose reports 1.12x the speed of the best safe Rust with 1.2-1.9x less memory. Designed by Wouter van Oortmerssen; the ~30k-line compiler was written by Claude Fable.
