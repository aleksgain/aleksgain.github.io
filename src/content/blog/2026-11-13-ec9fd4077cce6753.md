The model launch isn't the interesting part here — the router is. Google's Antigravity Orchestrator now switches between Gemini 4 Carbon and the new Hydrogen inside one session, reaching near-Carbon task success on Terminal-Bench 4.0 while using about 24% less quota and finishing about 19% faster. Hydrogen is $1.50/$7.50 per million tokens, flat across the full 1M context. Mixed-model agents are where the cost curve actually bends.
