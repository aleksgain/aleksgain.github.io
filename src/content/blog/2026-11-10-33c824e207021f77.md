Before anyone panics: 2048-bit RSA is fine, and nothing was factored here. What happened is that two DeepMind agents redid a known 1024-bit signature forgery in 391 CPU core-years instead of 1,380 — a 3.5x cut — in under four days, with roughly 20 messages from the human researcher that were mostly approvals. The thing worth filing away isn't that 1024-bit keys are weak. We knew that. It's who did the work.
