The best detail in Datadog's new SRE model isn't a benchmark. Their first reward function scored only the final answer — so Scout learned to make fewer claims and stop investigating early. They had to pay it for exploration and evidence too. Scout is a post-trained GLM-5.3 Flash; it resolved 80 of 90 SREGym faults at xhigh against 82 for GPT-6.1 Sol, at a fraction of the cost. It reads telemetry, suggests a cause, changes nothing.
