gVisor going to the CNCF is the part worth noting — a sandbox plenty of teams already depend on, now under vendor-neutral governance, donated alongside Agent Substrate. The number behind Substrate: agents sit idle 90-95% of the time, so it splits agent sessions from pre-warmed worker pools and resumes one in under 500 ms instead of creating a Pod per execution. Paying full price for idle agents is a real line item.
