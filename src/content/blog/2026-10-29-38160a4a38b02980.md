If you run a shared GPU cluster, Volcano v1.16 is the release where multi-tenancy stops being a quota spreadsheet. NamespaceQueue lets tenants build their own queue hierarchies inside a boundary admins authorize with spec.allowedNamespaces, and the new fairshare plugin tracks resource-seconds per namespace with a four-hour half-life decay — so yesterday's hog isn't punished forever. Both off by default. 65 contributors, K8s 1.36 supported.
