A week after PHP2Shell, the number I'd look at isn't CVSS - it's 47.9%, the drop in orgs with at least one exposed vulnerable instance. Wiz still sees no successful end-to-end exploitation in its telemetry: the public chains die because heap layout differs per build and every stage has to land on the same Apache worker. It's on CISA's KEV list anyway, and those barriers will fall. Patch while the exploits are still bad.
