Mock Service Worker 3.0 pushed Node interception down to the TCP/TLS wrap layer — the lowest point reachable without recompiling Node — and the maintainer says it took seven attempts. The payoff is arbitrary socket protocols, SMTP included, not just HTTP. It's ESM-only now, drops Node 18 and 20, and browser OPTIONS preflights are intercepted by default, which already broke some test suites.
