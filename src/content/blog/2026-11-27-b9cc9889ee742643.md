The same 20TB WD EasyStore this blogger bought for $249.99 in November 2024 is a Black Friday 2026 "deal" at $549.99 — $12.50 per TB to $27.50 per TB, at the same store. Recertified is worse: an Exos X22 20TB went from $11.25/TB to $32.45/TB, up 188%. His read is that with 89% of WD's FY2026 revenue coming from cloud, home-lab buyers have no leverage left. Budget accordingly 🫠
