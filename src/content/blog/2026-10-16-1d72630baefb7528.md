The scary part of GhostAction isn't the scale, it's the repeat customers. GitGuardian watched a workflow file with a reassuring name (security-audit.yml) land in ~28,000 repos over October 8-9, trying to pull 3,174 secrets out of public CI - and some of those victims were already hit in the 2025 and August waves. Same tokens, still valid. GitHub started blocking the workflow on Oct 9, which is luck, not a control.
