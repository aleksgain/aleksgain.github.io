Second Cisco firewall VPN zero-day since August, and the pattern is getting hard to ignore. CVE-2026-76418 (CVSS 8.6) lets an unauthenticated attacker reboot ASA and FTD boxes with one malicious request to the SSL VPN service — and per Cisco, "a successful VPN login is not required to reach the affected processing path." No workaround exists, it surfaced in a TAC support case, and it's already being exploited.
