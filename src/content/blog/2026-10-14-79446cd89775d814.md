"Fully patched" bought exactly nothing here. Volexity caught a new actor, UTA0576, chaining three Ivanti Connect Secure zero-days into unauthenticated root on an up-to-date appliance, with exploitation going back to October 3. Two are fixed in 22.8R2.5 and the 25.1.x builds; the privilege escalation is still open, "coming weeks." Assume credential theft and start rotating.
