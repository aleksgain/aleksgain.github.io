Formal verification has always been the thing you would love to do if you had three spare years. Anthropic and Theorem proved a no-crash property for 123 open-source libraries at the assembly level - 239 OSS-Fuzz entry points, about 180 million lines of Rocq, in a few weeks - and hundreds of bugs fell out along the way, including a rediscovery of CVE-2026-56132 in libexpat. The caveat: static single-threaded RISC-V against musl, not your x86 box.
