Strip away the crypto and this is a key-management story. Nobody broke Ethereum — the attacker got Binance's hot wallet signing system to sign for them across ten chains, with different key types and schemes, inside about half an hour. The clever part: 50 EIP-7702 authorizations pre-signed for 50 consecutive nonces, so one was certain to still be valid whenever the transaction landed. It matched, and 3m24s later the wallet was empty.
