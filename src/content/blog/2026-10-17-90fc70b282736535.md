robots.txt has always been an honour system; Cloudflare is adding a scoreboard. Verified bots now get evaluated continuously on whether they honour robots.txt and back off after an HTTP 429, and the verdict shows up as an explainable trust band instead of a one-time badge. Perplexity's 2025 delisting is what the old binary check missed. Scores land in Radar's bot directory, and customers pick their own threshold.
