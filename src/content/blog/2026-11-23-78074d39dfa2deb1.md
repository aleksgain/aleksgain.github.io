Zero Data Retention turns out to be a promise, not a control. Dragos seeded ~2 million prompts with canary links and credentials from July to November, then watched who came looking: three inference providers had been leaking request data for months without knowing. One breach exposed a couple hundred AWS keys. OpenRouter dropped all three and pulled 125 endpoints from ZDR eligibility. Assume the hop in the middle reads your prompts.
