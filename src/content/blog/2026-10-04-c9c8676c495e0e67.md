rclone took about 20 security disclosures in its first ten years, and more than 40 in the last month alone, per creator Nick Craig-Wood. Worse: a path-traversal fix in the OCaml compiler drew probe attempts in live server logs minutes after the PR went up. QEMU has already shortened its embargoes. Fixing in public is now a disclosure channel, and most projects have no process for that.
