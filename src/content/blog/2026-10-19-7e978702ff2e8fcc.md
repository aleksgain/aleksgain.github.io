US cybersecurity job postings hit a four-year low: 235,786 for Jan-Sep, down 26.4%. Underneath that, AI Security Engineer demand is up 268.3% and Cyber Data Scientist up 203.5%, while Penetration Tester fell 45.9% and Threat Hunter 40%. CyberSN maps postings by the work described rather than the title, which makes this harder to wave away as renaming. Less a shrinking field, more a violently reshuffled one.
