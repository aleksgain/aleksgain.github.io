The n-day race just lost its human bottleneck. Google's threat intel team found a framework called exploit_agent on an exposed staging server: it watches popular web apps, diffs the code before and after each patch, drafts an exploit and runs it — no human in the loop, which GTIG says it hasn't seen before. Zero-day exploitation went from roughly 8 a month in 2025 to 11 in 2026, spiking to 22 in August. Plan patch windows accordingly.
