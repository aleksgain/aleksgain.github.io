Upload a .png, get a shell. CVE-2026-125396 is a use-after-free in Ghostscript's font loader that disables the -dSAFER sandbox — and you rarely call Ghostscript directly: ImageMagick, Pillow and libvips do it for you, whatever the file extension says. pwn.ai landed it via Roundcube attachments, a shared Debian print queue and two WordPress plugins' upload forms. Fixed in 10.08.0 in September; several distros were still catching up.
