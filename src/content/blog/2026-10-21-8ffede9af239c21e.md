Eight eMudhra intermediate CA keys are compromised in the most avoidable way there is: the CA generated them badly, so the private key can be recovered from the certificate - and could be since April 2025. Chrome and Firefox blocked them Oct 17, Apple Oct 19, Microsoft Oct 20. Certificate Transparency kept browsers safe; everything that doesn't enforce CT is the problem. Patched: Go 1.27.3, certifi 2026.10.19, curl bundle 2026-10-20.
