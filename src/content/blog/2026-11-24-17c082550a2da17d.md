The v1.38 sneak peek is out, and the line to read twice isn't a feature. The kubelet's cgroupDriver field is deprecated (KEP-4033): the driver now comes from CRI, which means the container runtime has to be up before the kubelet starts, or it exits before registering the node. kubeadm stops defaulting to systemd too. Pod-level resources and kuberc go stable, and a new EvictionRequest API lands in alpha. Check your node bootstrap ordering first.
