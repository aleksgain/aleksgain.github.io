Your TLS trust chain is only as strong as the registry sitting above you. Attackers breached third-party operators behind the .GH, .AS and .SL registries, rewrote authoritative DNS, and walked out with valid HTTPS certificates for Google domains - no compromise of Google's own systems required. Chrome has blocked the certs via CRLSets, which is great if everyone you care about uses Chrome. Go read your CT logs, parked domains included.
