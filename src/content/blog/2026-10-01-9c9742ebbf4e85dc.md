Truffle Security scanned an LLM training dataset of 224 million repos and 58 billion files and pulled out 543,699 credentials that still worked. The bit that stings: median exposure was 784 days, and 36.8% were pushed after GitHub turned Push Protection on by default. npm had 1 valid token out of 101,886; Google Cloud had 69,041 of 126,963 still working. Detection isn't the gap, rotation and expiry are.
