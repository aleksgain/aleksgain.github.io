Google just put $250,000 on the table for anyone who can demonstrate roles/owner or full IAM control over arbitrary GCP organizations — with no pre-existing project access, no stolen credentials and no social engineering in the chain. RCE in Borg counts too, on the condition that you stop probing the moment you get code execution. A quarter million is a cheap way to find out your tenant boundary leaks before someone else does.
