Four TPU systems in a single year is a strange cadence for hardware. The interesting one is 8tx: TPU 8t silicon on 5th-gen AMD EPYC hosts plus hardware confidential compute, so weights and data stay isolated during processing — that's what unblocks regulated inference. Google also says its TPU Sync software cuts time to first token by more than 90%, which I'd want to see on someone else's benchmark.
