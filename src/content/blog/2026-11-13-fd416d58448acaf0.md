The most useful line in Microsoft's outage updates: load "isn't driven by an increase in traffic, but by a number of servers being unexpectedly unavailable". Lose capacity and the survivors look overloaded. Microsoft later said a core process on the affected servers was getting overwhelmed, and early recovery fell short. It began 1:26 PM ET Thursday, hitting Exchange Online, Teams, Copilot and Defender XDR. Retry budgets, anyone? 😅
