Your firewall management server is a juicier target than the firewall. GreyNoise is tracking a campaign it calls GT13 into at least 22 organisations: an auth bypass in Check Point SmartConsole (CVE-2026-16232) used to run tasks on 24 management servers, copy the firewall database and internal CA, mint a certificate, and unload security policies. Last activity 2 November, and they think it's ongoing. If you run one, treat that CA as burned.
