A single ~516 KB HTTP/3 stream that pins a server at 100% CPU indefinitely is a good reminder that a limit on compressed input is not a limit at all. FoxIO found the same QPACK decompression weakness in 7 of the 10 stacks it tested: about 4,000x memory amplification on Bun, 600-1,800x on lsquic. Patched in quic-go 0.57.0, quiche 0.29.3, lsquic 4.9.0 - Bun, hyperium h3 and aioquic still open, with PoCs public.
