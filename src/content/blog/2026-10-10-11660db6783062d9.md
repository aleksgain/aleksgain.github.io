Cloudflare acquired Deno, and the fine print matters more than the headline: the runtime gets one more year of monthly bug and security releases before development stops, and Deno Deploy shuts down in six months. JSR survives, moving onto Cloudflare's infrastructure. Ryan Dahl's new job is making self-hosted workerd a supported way to run Workers apps — the more interesting half of the deal, honestly.
