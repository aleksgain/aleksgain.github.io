A stray "=" at the end of a Base64 string is now a pre-auth RCE. CVE-2026-114938 (CVSS 9.2) is an out-of-bounds write in OpenJDK's hand-written x86-64 Base64 decoder: a 50-byte buffer takes a 64-byte write. Hits OpenJDK 17.0.6+ on AVX-512 VBMI CPUs, with working exploits against default-config Keycloak, Jira, Confluence, Jenkins and Artifactory. Can't patch today? -XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics buys time.
