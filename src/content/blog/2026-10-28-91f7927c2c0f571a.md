Two years of CPython JIT work ends not with a benchmark argument but a bandwidth one. PEP 836 was withdrawn on October 19 after the steering council asked for a unified design, continuous compatibility checks rather than a review pencilled in for August 2028, and a real security story. The authors' own words: not enough personal bandwidth to carry it forward. Removal lands early in the 3.16 cycle, and the reference implementation stays published.
