This one sat in attackers' hands for over two months before a fix existed. CVE-2026-123890 (CVSS 9.8) is an auth bypass in vCenter's Directory Service: unauthenticated straight to SSO admin. Mandiant ties it to Armory Castle (ex-UNC5221), who used a tool called Bonesaw to lift SAML signing material, forge tokens and walk over to ESXi. vCenter 7.0 gets no patch at all. So patching isn't the whole job here — go look for evidence of compromise.
