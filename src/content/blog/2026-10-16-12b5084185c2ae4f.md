NGINX shipped its eighth security release of the year: five bugs, three of them memory corruption in the worker process that F5 scores 9.2. The one I'd read twice is CVE-2026-97629 - with a shared ssl_session_cache and no explicit ssl_session_ticket_key, rotation copied two of three key slots, so a forged session could skip client-certificate verification. Fixed in 1.30.6 and 1.31.7. On 1.28 there's no fix; you move.
