Another edge appliance, another quiet zero-day. CVE-2026-102764 is an auth bypass in BeyondTrust Remote Support 26.2.3 and earlier, exploited since late August by a China-nexus actor: web shell, SUID escalation to root, Sawfly plus a tunneler, logs wiped. Shadowserver counts 1,400+ exposed instances. Mandiant's line is the one to take seriously — if yours was reachable in that window, treat it as compromised, not merely patched.
