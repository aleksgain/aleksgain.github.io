Perpetual licences are only perpetual until the vendor stops supporting them. Tesco bought VMware in 2021, Broadcom ended support in January 2026, and now Tesco pays third parties while hiring someone to move its in-store Edge Platform to Proxmox. Its lawsuit says end of 2027 is the earliest it can be off VMware, and calls that a significant risk. Two years to leave a hypervisor is worth remembering before the next contract.
