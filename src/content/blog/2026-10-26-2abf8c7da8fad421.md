The interesting part of Google and Meta's TorchTPU release isn't the one-line model.to("tpu") - it's the regression suite behind it. They ran 4,917 Hugging Face models end to end: 98% executed without crashing, and 638 of 657 architecture representatives matched GPU numerics. Google is also upfront that the one-liner gets a model running, not running well - sharding and kernels are still your problem.
