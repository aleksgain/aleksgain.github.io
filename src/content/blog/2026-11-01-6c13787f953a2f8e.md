The interesting part of Google's PageBreak isn't the Gemini models hunting bugs — it's the rule that nothing reaches a product team until a deterministic validator reproduces the exploit: executing JS for XSS, writing to a world-readable path for traversal, outbound DNS for RCE. 500+ XSS bugs across Google's web apps since January, with a near-zero false positive rate. That's the pattern worth copying.
