Lima has quietly turned into the agent sandbox nobody marketed as one. v2.3 adds an hcs driver that drives Windows' Host Compute System API directly — the same one WSL2 sits on, no QEMU needed — plus real bridged networking on Linux. The sandboxing bits are the interesting part: --sync-exclude and .limasyncignore keep .env and .git on the host, and hostLoopback: false stops the guest reaching your localhost.
